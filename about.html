🪙 About Actifo

Social Detox,
Gamified.

We think the fix for endless scrolling isn't willpower — it's a better feedback loop.

In a world of endless scrolling and digital distractions, we believe in reclaiming your focus and time. Actifo was born from a simple idea: what if managing your digital habits could be not just easy, but genuinely fun and rewarding?

Our goal is to help you build a healthier relationship with technology. We've taken the principles of social detox and turned them into an engaging, gamified experience — friendly competition, rewarding challenges, and honest analytics that show you exactly where your time goes.

We're not about disconnecting completely. We're about connecting more meaningfully with your own goals, and with the people cheering you on. Every challenge you join, every coin you earn, is a small vote for the version of your day you actually want.
