Privacy & security

Your habits are yours. Period.

We built Actifo to help you build better habits, not to build a profile on you. Here's exactly what that means.

We see the clock, not the content

We know you spent 30 minutes on Instagram — never what you did there. No messages, photos, contacts or browsing history, ever.

Encrypted, always

Stored securely in Google Cloud with industry-standard encryption, in transit and at rest.

Yours alone

Every account can only ever access its own data. Nobody else's, ever.

Deletable, on request

Pause tracking anytime in-app, or email us to delete your data outright.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you see what I do inside apps?

No. We only see how long you're in an app — say, 30 minutes on Instagram — never what you scrolled, liked, watched or messaged. Read the full privacy policy →

Does tracking drain my battery?

No. Actifo reads the usage stats your phone's OS already tracks — it doesn't run a background service watching your screen, so there's no meaningful battery cost.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Your usage data is automatically deleted after 90 days regardless. Want it gone sooner? Email us and we'll delete it outright.

What can my friend see when we QuickCompare?

Just the comparison itself — who spent less time on the app you're competing over. They never see your full usage history, your other apps, or anything else in your account.

Are coins worth real money?

Coins aren't currency — you earn them through challenges, mystery boxes and QuickCompare wins, then redeem them for real rewards like Amazon, Myntra and Swiggy vouchers in the Reward Store.

Is Actifo on iPhone yet?

Not yet — we're Android-first for now, with iOS coming soon. Join on Android today and we'll let you know the moment iOS is ready.
