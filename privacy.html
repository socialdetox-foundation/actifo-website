Privacy & security

Your habits are yours. Period.

We built Actifo to help you build better habits, not to build a profile on you. Here's exactly what that means.

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Only what's needed

App usage stats and basic account info — never your messages, photos, contacts or browsing history.

Encrypted, always

Stored securely in Google Cloud with industry-standard encryption, in transit and at rest.

Yours alone

Every account can only ever access its own data. Nobody else's, ever.

Deletable, on request

Pause tracking anytime in-app, or email us to delete your data outright.

This policy covers what we collect, why, how it's stored, and how you stay in control of it. If anything here is unclear, email us — we'd rather explain it plainly than bury it in legal language.

1. What we collect

  • App usage statistics — the apps you use and how much time you spend on them each day, so Actifo can show you insights and power challenges.
  • Account information — your email or sign-in method (Google or similar), used to create and secure your account.

We do not collect your messages, photos, contacts, or browsing history.

2. Why we collect it

  • To show you insights into your daily app usage.
  • To power features like challenges, QuickCompare and rewards.
  • To help you track progress over time.

3. How your data is stored

  • Stored securely in Google Cloud with industry-standard encryption, both in transit and at rest.
  • Each account can only ever access its own data.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

4. Your control

  • Pause tracking anytime from within the app.
  • Request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at socialdetox.foundation@gmail.com.

We plan to offer an in-app "Delete My Data" button soon.

5. Data retention

We keep your app usage data for up to 90 days to show trends and history. After that, it's automatically deleted.

6. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at socialdetox.foundation@gmail.com — see our Contact page for more ways to get in touch.
